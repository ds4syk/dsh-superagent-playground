Pose-estimation layers that map learned quantities to a 6-DoF pose are usually built for equality constraints, yet physical admissibility is expressed by inequalities: positive depth in front of the camera, non-penetration of a manipulated object and its support, and field-of-view or tilt limits. Adding $p$ such constraints to a semidefinite-programming layer inflates the lifted matrix from $10\times10$ to $(10+p)\times(10+p)$ and destroys strict complementarity. We present \textbf{Constrained-QPEPLayer}, a differentiable 6-DoF pose layer for inequality-constrained quaternion pose estimation built from three ingredients. (i)~An \emph{additive constant-dimension reduction}: for the Rockafellar--Wets augmented Lagrangian of the constrained quaternion pose problem, every inner subproblem collapses, after an exact $3\times3$ Schur complement in the translation, to a fixed-dimension quartic form on $\mathbb{S}^3$ independent of $p$. (ii)~A \emph{sound posteriori certificate}: a $14\times14$ Veronese-augmented Shor relaxation whose PSD block does not grow with $p$, together with a dual-repair theorem that makes every reported gap non-negative by construction. (iii)~\emph{Fischer--Burmeister reduced implicit differentiation}, whose $(6+|\mathcal A|)\times(6+|\mathcal A|)$ saddle-point system stays invertible at rank-deficient contacts. The layer returns pose, certificate value and feasibility violation, and flags slack relaxations. On synthetic desktop instances it is 100\% feasible with 0.00~mm penetration, against 103.02~mm median for the unconstrained solve; on BOP LINEMOD, LINEMOD-Occlusion and YCB-Video ($n=90/72/84$) rigorous certification rises from 24.4/15.3/13.1\% to 51.1/41.7/23.8\% with a sound bound-RLT family. Hard constraints thus deliver admissibility and certification at a cost independent of $p$.
