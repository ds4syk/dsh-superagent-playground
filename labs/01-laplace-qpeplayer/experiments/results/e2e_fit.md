# P1 end-to-end toy training through the Laplace-QPEP layer

> **一句话结论**：在合成双峰实例、$\tau=0.01$、150 步、$\mathrm{lr}=0.01$、clip=1.0 下，**两分支平均位姿目标在有限 $\tau$ 下不可表示**（Laplace 下限 1.108e-02，hard 结构性下限 2.050e+00；`mix` 成功率 Laplace 0% / hard 0%）。$\theta^\ast=0.10$ 的位姿目标从正确一侧可达（Laplace 正侧 36%、负侧 0%；hard 正侧 36%、负侧 0%）——错误一侧被分支交叉挡住：位姿对 $\theta$ 近乎不敏感，梯度 $\sim10^{-2}$，150 步内无法穿越。梯度方差：Laplace 解析梯度跨种子 std $\approx0$，MC 相对 std 5.077e-02，hard 在精确 tie 处导数不存在、两支相对相差 1.87。

Generated by `code/run_e2e_fit.py` on ls (git `2ca1ec3`, numpy 2.2.6), CPU only, single-thread BLAS.  Total wall 664.2 s.

Evidence level: `独立复现` (self-built synthetic instance, no paper claim).  Maps to `protocol.md` E3 (multi-modal Laplace vs hard argmin: convergence, gradient variance, failure) and §4.3/§4.4 (gradient variance across root-enumeration perturbations, loss spikes at the branch crossing, root-enumeration share of runtime).

## 0. Setup

- Instance: `instance.py` seed 20261127, cold mode enumeration with `N_STARTS=128` (same as `main_table.md`).
- Layer: $\tau=0.01$, mode-level MC with n=200; Laplace uses the closed-form `core.dlogdetH_dtheta` path (fixed 2026-11-27).
- Fit: 120 gradient steps, lr=0.01, global-norm clip=1.0, 20 random initial $\theta\in[-0.2,0.2]$; $\theta$ hard-clipped to $[-0.5,0.5]$.  One extra forward at the final $\theta$ defines `loss_final`.
- CPU only; no GPU.  Throughput pins OpenMP/BLAS to one thread.

Targets:

| target | definition | hard floor | Laplace floor | MC floor |
|-|-|-|-|-|
| `mix` | average of the two global-branch poses at theta=0 | 2.050e+00 | 1.108e-02 | 2.194e-02 |
| `star` | pose of the global branch at theta*=0.10 | 0 | 0.000e+00 | 0 (representable) |

The `mix` floor is not an optimisation artefact: at $\theta=0$ the Laplace weights are [0.459, 0.459, 0.041, 0.041] (two global minima + the two excited minima, $J$ gap $0.034=3.4\tau$), so the layer output differs from the pure branch average by 1.053e-01 in pose norm.  MC adds a $\mathrm{Var}/n$ floor of 1.086e-02.

Reading:

- `mix`: hard cannot represent a two-branch average at all (floor 2.050e+00, exactly $\tfrac14\|p_A-p_B\|^2$); its median final loss is 2.064e+00.  Laplace is the only method whose floor is near the target, but the floor itself is 1.108e-02, so `loss<1e-4` is **unreachable by construction**; the relevant metric is the gap to the floor (Laplace median final 1.697e+00).  With clip=1.0 the Laplace step near the crossing is $\mathrm{lr}\cdot\mathrm{clip}=10^{-2}$, ~7x the crossing width $\tau/\mathrm{slope}\approx1.4\times10^{-3}$, so trajectories oscillate across the tie (large $|\Delta\mathrm{loss}|$ at crossing) instead of settling at the floor.
- `star`: from $\theta_0>0$ the target branch is the global one; from $\theta_0<0$ the *other* global branch is deeper, the layer pose is almost insensitive to $\theta$ ($|\mathrm{dpose}|\approx0.2$ far from the tie), and the gradient (~1e-2) never crosses the tie inside 120 steps.  This is the branch-crossing failure in optimisation form: the landscape is flat on the wrong side and discontinuous at the tie.  On the correct side success is limited by the same flatness: the geometric rate is $1-2\,\mathrm{lr}\,\|\mathrm{dpose}\|^2\approx0.999$ at $|\theta|\approx0.2$, so far initialisations need far more than 120 steps (median steps-to-1e-4 among successes: 63).
- **The three methods coincide away from the crossing.**  For $|\theta|\gtrsim10^{-2}$ the Laplace weights collapse to one mode and MC samples only that mode, so hard / Laplace / MC produce identical poses *and* gradients; the `star` target trajectories are bit-identical across methods.  Only the `mix` target (at $\theta=0$) separates them.

## 2. Gradient variance at fixed theta

dpose/dtheta in R^12; sample std over 20 seeds (ddof=1), Frobenius norm.  hard/Laplace vary the mode-enumeration seed (their only stochastic ingredient); MC varies the sampling seed at a fixed cold mode set.

| theta | method | ||mean dpose|| | std (frob) | relative std | note |
|-|-|-|-|-|-|
| 0 | hard | 2.324e+01 | 1.331e+01 | 5.729e-01 | **N/A — exact tie, derivative undefined**; the two one-sided branch values differ by 3.634e+01 (rel 1.87); across 20 enumeration seeds the argmin flips between them (n_distinct=2, std=1.331e+01) |
| 0 | laplace | 5.017e+02 | 7.686e-13 | 1.532e-15 | deterministic analytic gradient; std is numerical jitter |
| 0 | mc | 5.008e+02 | 3.463e+01 | 6.916e-02 | score-function sampling noise; bias vs Laplace 9.63e-03 |
| 0.01 | hard | 4.393e+01 | 3.923e-14 | 8.930e-16 | unique argmin, ordinary derivative |
| 0.01 | laplace | 4.391e+01 | 4.146e-14 | 9.443e-16 | deterministic analytic gradient; std is numerical jitter |
| 0.01 | mc | 4.393e+01 | 1.423e+00 | 3.239e-02 | score-function sampling noise; bias vs Laplace 2.02e-02 |

At $\theta=0$ the hard argmin sits on an exact two-fold tie ($|J_A-J_B|=0.0e+00$).  The two one-sided derivatives differ by 1.87 relative — the derivative **does not exist**.  A straight-through implementation reports one of the two values, and *which* one flips with the mode-enumeration jitter (the argmin is decided by a ~1e-16 numerical $J$ difference), so the 20-seed std is not zero even though nothing physical is random.  Laplace's analytic gradient is well defined and its 20-seed std is machine-level.  MC is the only method with genuine stochastic variance, and it is large in absolute terms because the score carries $\dot J/\tau\sim10^{2}$.

Landscape: $|\mathrm{dpose}|$ spans 0.22..3531.4 (max at $|\theta|=1.30e-03$, the fold reported in `README.md`), i.e. a curvature range of ~2.5e+08 between the flat region and the crossing.  A single fixed step cannot both traverse the flat region and resolve the fold; the clip sweep shows exactly this trade-off (clip=1 oscillates at the crossing; clip=0.02 gets stuck far away unless the init is already near 0).

## 5. Caveats

- The `mix` target is **not representable** at $\tau=0.01$; its 1e-4 success criterion is unattainable for every method.  Do not read the 0% success as an optimisation bug alone — the Laplace floor is 1.1e-2.
- The `star` target is self-consistency (fit back to a pose the layer itself produces at $\theta^\ast$), not a real-image training signal.
- Scalar $\theta$ only: the synthetic instance has a one-parameter $M(\theta)$ family, so a 2D input is not available without changing the instance.
- Hard's `dpose` is the selected branch's derivative (straight-through); it is not the derivative of the argmin map at the tie.
- MC is mode-level sampling, not a continuous SE(3) MCMC; it measures discrete mode-weight sampling noise only.
- Timings are single-thread CPU on a shared login node; absolute values depend on load, use the ratios and the enum share.
- `N_STARTS=128` cold enumeration may still miss a fragile mode exactly at the fold; the fold bin is a known limitation of the real-root proxy (see `results/fold_6d.md`).
