Differentiable pose-estimation layers promise to close the loop between perception and control, but existing layers are either \emph{local} (Gauss--Newton/Levenberg--Marquardt with implicit differentiation) or built on a convex relaxation that must remain tight, and neither handles the inequality constraints that make a pose physically admissible: positive depth in front of the camera, non-penetration of a manipulated object with its support surface, field-of-view and tilt-cone limits. Adding $p$ such constraints to a semidefinite-programming layer inflates the lifted matrix from $10\times10$ to $(10+p)\times(10+p)$ and destroys strict complementarity. We present \textbf{Constrained-QPEPLayer}, a differentiable 6-DoF pose layer for inequality-constrained quaternion pose estimation. Its ingredients are (i) an \emph{additive constant-dimension reduction}: for the Rockafellar--Wets augmented Lagrangian of the constrained quaternion pose problem (C-QPEP), every inner subproblem collapses, after an exact $3\times3$ Schur complement in the translation, to a fixed-dimension quartic form on $\mathbb{S}^3$ independent of $p$; (ii) a \emph{sound posteriori certificate} given by a $14\times14$ Veronese-augmented Shor relaxation whose PSD block does not grow with $p$, together with a dual-repair theorem (P2-Cert) that makes every reported gap non-negative by construction; and (iii) \emph{Fischer--Burmeister (FB) reduced implicit differentiation} whose $(6+|\mathcal A|)\times(6+|\mathcal A|)$ saddle-point system stays invertible at rank-deficient contacts, with an $\varepsilon\to0$ limit that is a \emph{selected} generalized derivative. The layer is fail-safe certifiable rather than certifiable: it reports ``uncertified'' when the relaxation is slack. On synthetic desktop instances it is 100\% feasible with 0.00~mm penetration, against a median 103.02~mm for the unconstrained solve; on BOP LINEMOD/LINEMOD-Occlusion/YCB-Video ($n=90/72/84$, ground-truth-projected correspondences) rigorous certification rises from 24.4/15.3/13.1\% to 51.1/41.7/23.8\% with a sound bound-RLT family. No detector, learned baseline or end-to-end training is claimed.
