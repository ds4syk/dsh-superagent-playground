\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}
We presented Constrained-QPEPLayer, a differentiable 6-DoF pose layer for inequality-constrained quaternion pose estimation. It reduces every ALM inner subproblem to a fixed-dimension quartic form on $\mathbb{S}^3$, certifies global optimality a posteriori with a $14\times14$ relaxation whose PSD block is independent of $p$, and differentiates through an FB-regularised saddle-point system that stays invertible at rank-deficient and weakly active contacts. The certificate carries a dual repair that makes every reported gap non-negative, on a controlled instance where the dual-function gap is itself negative ($-1.67\times10^{-11}$) and the relaxation is tight to $2.2\times10^{-12}$. On real data the certificate is tight on 24.4/15.3/13.1\% of poses with base cuts and 51.1/41.7/23.8\% with sound bound-RLT (Table~\ref{tab:rigorous}), the remaining rows failing by relaxation slack rather than solver error.
