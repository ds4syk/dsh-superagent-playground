\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}
We presented Constrained-QPEPLayer, a differentiable 6-DoF pose layer for inequality-constrained quaternion pose estimation. It reduces every ALM inner subproblem to a fixed-dimension quartic form on $\mathbb{S}^3$, certifies global optimality a posteriori with a $14\times14$ relaxation whose PSD block is independent of $p$, and differentiates through an FB-regularised saddle-point system that stays invertible at rank-deficient and weakly active contacts. We characterised the $\varepsilon\to0$ limit---an $H$-weighted minimum-norm solution, exact under a compatibility condition and a \emph{selected} generalized derivative otherwise---and replaced an earlier ``100\% certification'' claim with a conditional theorem plus a sound relaxation that is tight to $2\times10^{-11}$ on a controlled instance where the dual-function gap is negative. Two open questions are settled (the $4/12/16/32/40$ root spectrum with the generic 40 intact at rank 9, and a dual-repair bound that cannot overshoot); the generalized derivative at weak-active points remains open. On real data the certificate is tight on 24.4/15.3/13.1\% of poses with base cuts and 51.1/41.7/23.8\% with sound bound-RLT (Table~\ref{tab:rigorous}), the remaining rows failing by a median gap of $10^{-5}$, i.e.\ by relaxation slack rather than solver error.
